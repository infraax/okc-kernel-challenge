### Critique of L-A1 ("Light L2 Weight Prefetch Before `gdc_wait`" in [itachi-corridor.md](file://<workspace>#L38-L43))
- **Would it work on Hopper?** Mechanics-wise **yes** (`sm_90` schedules dependent CTAs onto freed SM slots once the predecessor executes `griddepcontrol.launch_dependents`, and instructions before `griddepcontrol.wait` run legally when touching only immutable weights), **but its net gain on this `T = 1` chain is capped at ~1–3 µs (est.) by four hard limits:**
  1. **Broken PDL chain (library kernels):** `nvjet` (`qkv_gate`, `splitKreduce`, `o`) and SGLang C++ kernels (`attn_boundary`, `ffn_boundary`, `routing_kernel`) cannot emit `gdc_launch_dependents` or pre-wait prefetches, breaking over half the 16 handovers; meanwhile [`w4_smallm_kernel`](file://<workspace>#L1969-L1990) *already* uses PDL.
  2. **Triton software-pipeliner hoisting hazard:** With `num_stages = 3..4`, Triton hoists prologue `cp.async` loads of **both** `w_ptr` and `x_ptr` to the top of the kernel; unless `gdc_wait` acts as a compiler barrier dominating `tl.load(x_ptr)`, Triton will hoist activation loads *above* `gdc_wait` (data race) or sink weight prefetches *below* it.
  3. **Premature trigger / SM starvation (already hurting GEMM1→GEMM2):** If `gdc_launch_dependents` fires while the producer still needs SMs or HBM (as in [`w4_smallm_kernel`](file://<workspace>#L1970)), dependent CTAs grab free SM/smem slots, steal DRAM bandwidth during the producer's `.cg` split-K reduction tail, and sit blocked on `gdc_wait`.
  4. **L2 prefetch hint dropping:** Non-binding `prefetch.global.L2` is dropped under L1/TEX MSHR pressure unless backed by actual register/smem loads.
