### 1.2 Anomalies
- **Constant +3.8–4.4 µs offset between `ours` and `p10–p90` on every shape** (e.g., #0 `ours=14.0`, `p10–p90=17.5–18.7`): The judge subtracts ~4 µs fixed graph-replay cost from `ours` ([TASK.md](problems/muse_bf16_proj/TASK.md#L93)) but leaves `p10–p90` unadjusted.
- **Negative `idle` on single-kernel shapes (#2 -0.2 µs, #22 -0.4 µs, #23 -0.9 µs, #28 -0.3 µs)** vs **large positive `idle` (2.8–4.4 µs) on 2-kernel split-K shapes (#0, #5, #10, #15)**: Single-kernel graph overhead is ~3.2–3.6 µs (so subtracting 4 µs makes `wall < Σkernels`), whereas 2-kernel cuBLAS split-K incurs an unprofiled async workspace memset / inter-kernel barrier bubble of 2.8–4.4 µs.
- **Shape #19 (`router`, $m=8$) SGLang spike (26.2 µs vs 10.2 µs at $m=1$, 15.0 µs at $m=16$)**: SGLang runs FP32 `F.linear` up to $m=8$ before switching to split BF16 GEMMs at $m>8$, inflating our #19 speedup to 3.894×.

- **cuBLAS `qkv_gate` / `unpooler` / `pooler` microbenchmark**: Level-1 trace comparing (a) cuBLAS default vs (b) [`_gemv_splitk`](kernels/muse_bf16_proj/director_v1b.py#L44-L70) with `SPLIT=1..4` and in-kernel semaphore reduction (no memset/reduce launch) on #3/#18 (`unpooler` $n=1024$) and #0/#5/#10/#15 (`qkv_gate`).

- **Merged cuBLAS call counts (`calls = 2` on #2–#5, #8–#11)**: `o` and `unpooler` select the same `nvjet_sm90_tst_*` kernel symbol, summing both projections into one table row.

### 4.2 Anomalies
- **Wildly swinging `idle` (+51.7 µs / +11.0% on #4 vs −35.7 µs on #5 and −19.6 µs on #2) with zero multi-stream overlap**: Paired with the huge **40–111 µs `p10–p90` spreads** (#1 `112.9–167.4`, #2 `174.3–247.6`, #4 `418.9–529.4`, #5 `568.2–678.9`), this proves the separate CUPTI profiling pass sampled a light-routing seed on #4 (`kernels Σ = 417.6 µs` $\approx$ `p10 = 418.9 µs`) and heavy-routing seeds on #2 and #5 (`kernels Σ = 653.3 µs` near `p90 = 678.9 µs`). Each $\pm 1$ active expert shifts time by ~14–20 µs.
- **Misplaced `pdl_trigger()` in [`w4_smallm_kernel`](kernels/muse_moe_w4a16/sonnet_v3b.py#L552-L553)**: `if (FUSE) pdl_trigger();` fires at line 553 *before* GEMM1's `z`-loop, while GEMM2's `if (!FUSE) pdl_wait();` (line 573) still blocks on full GEMM1 completion, and [`_epilogue_kernel`](kernels/muse_moe_w4a16/sonnet_v3b.py#L147-L178) lacks `launch_pdl` entirely (explaining the 5.6 µs idle on #0).
