I need a deep, source-backed research report: papers, GitHub repos, Hugging Face kernels/blogs, vendor docs, conference talks. Only openly published work; do NOT look for or include other participants' challenge submissions.

The four problems and our current bottlenecks:
1. MoE expert GEMMs, W4A16: weights in NVFP4 (E2M1 values + FP8 E4M3 scales per 16 elements), BF16 activations, top-8 routing over 256 experts with expert parallelism (32 local experts), hidden 8192, intermediate 4096 (gate+up fused, SiLU, down). Batch 1–256 tokens → 1–77 tokens per expert. Our CUDA kernel (mma.sync m16n8k16, register double-buffered weight loads) only reaches ~1.6–2.1 TB/s; Marlin ~2.5 TB/s.
2. Attention decode: FP8 E4M3 paged KV cache with page size 1 (every token's row is a random 64–128 B gather), GQA 16 query heads / 2 KV heads (head dim 128 assumed), sliding window 2048 on some layers, full 80k context on others, decode (q_len 1) and speculative verify (q_len 8), batch 1–32. Our weak spot: verify on full layers runs at ~1.7 TB/s (exp/convert/tensor bound), decode-full ~3.1 TB/s.
3. Dense BF16 projections (GEMV / skinny GEMM, M = 1–256, e.g. 8192×4096, 8192×1024): cuBLAS and our Triton at ~70% of the bandwidth floor; wave quantisation at M = 128/256.
4. A whole decoder layer (~20 kernels: QKV proj, attention, O proj, norms, pooler/router GEMVs, routing, MoE, epilogue, unpooler): fusion and launch/gap reduction.
